    What these results mean

    These results compare one approximate point with modelled flood extents. They are not live conditions, a flood-risk rating, a water-depth estimate or an official property assessment.

    Outside a modelled extent does not mean safe. Official assessment coverage has not been supplied, so an unshaded point may be beyond the study coverage. Missing or unavailable data cannot be treated as an outside result.

    The scenarios come from separate studies with different dates and recorded upstream limits. Their differences are not a like-for-like measure of climate change.

    1% AEP means a flood of that size or larger has a 1% chance of occurring in any given year. It does not mean flooding happens only once every 100 years.

    Optional prototype layers are not used for these results: the planning overlay is a merged SBO/LSIO boundary, not a confirmed 2100 scenario, and the synthetic demo is not an observed 2010 flood extent.
