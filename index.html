    What these results mean

    These results compare one approximate point with the three original prototype layers, including layers hidden on the map. They are not live conditions, a flood-risk rating, a water-depth estimate or an official property assessment.

    “2100” contains merged SBO/LSIO planning boundaries. “Current day” and “2010” contain synthetic shapes derived from those boundaries, not actual present-day or historic flooding.

    The original layer names are retained for prototype continuity; they do not validate a year, event or probability. Differences between these shapes are not evidence of climate change.

    Outside a displayed area does not mean safe, and missing data cannot be treated as an outside result. The catchment boundary is not an assessment-coverage boundary.

    The separate official baseline and 2100 model snapshots are not loaded or used in this version.

    Data sources and limitations

    The original names are retained for the team's prototype. They are not verification of a year, historical event or 1% AEP probability.

    These layers do not provide water depth, a risk rating or real-time conditions. Address lookup checks whether a single approximate point intersects these same prototype shapes.
