Data sources and limitations

1% AEP means 1% annual exceedance probability, not a flood that happens only once every 100 years.

These are separate studies with different dates and recorded upstream limits. Their differences are not a like-for-like measure of climate change.

These layers do not provide water depth, a risk rating or real-time conditions. Address search only locates a place; it does not assess its flood risk.

Optional prototype layers: the planning overlay is a merged SBO/LSIO boundary, not a confirmed 2100 scenario. The synthetic demo is not an observed 2010 flood extent.
